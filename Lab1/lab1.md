Exercise 1: System Hardware Detective

System Specification Sheet

- Processor (CPU) brand, model, and number of cores: AMD Ryzen AI 350 / Radeon 860M, 8 Cores (16 Logical Processors)
- Total RAM amount and type: 16 GB DDR5 (2 x 8GB Micron, 5600 MT/s)
- Storage device type and capacity: SSD, 512 GB (SKHynix_HFS512GEM4X182N)

Analysis Questions:

1. Based on your CPU cores, how many tasks can your processor theoretically handle simultaneously?
   16 tasks (8 cores, 16 logical processors).

2. Is your storage primarily HDD or SSD? What are the performance implications?
   SSD. Faster boot, app loading, and file transfers. More durable (no moving parts).

3. How does your RAM amount compare to typical requirements for modern applications?
   16 GB meets typical modern needs. Good for multitasking, office work, light gaming. Heavy gaming/editing may need 32 GB+.
