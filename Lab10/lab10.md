## 3. Conclusion

After researching these five projects, I learned that different software licenses provide different levels of freedom. The MIT License allows commercial use, modification, and redistribution with basic attribution requirements. CC0 provides even more freedom by waiving copyright as much as legally possible. Microsoft Azure uses its own agreement, which has specific terms that users must follow.

This research helped me understand why checking a project's license is important before using or sharing its software.
