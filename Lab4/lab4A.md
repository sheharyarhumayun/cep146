# Exercise 3: Academic Integrity Policy

# Seneca Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose
To maintain a high standard of **academic integrity** across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning and working practices.

## Key Definitions

> **Academic integrity** is "a commitment, even in the face of adversity, to six fundamental values: honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility and courage."

- **Cheating**: Obtaining or attempting to obtain credit for work by dishonest or deceptive means.
- **Plagiarism**: Using another individual's work and presenting it as one's own, without properly citing the source.
- **Contract cheating**: A student's academic work is completed by a third party on their behalf.
- **Falsification**: Misrepresenting or forging documentation to gain an academic advantage.
- **Impersonation**: Taking a test or assessment on another individual's behalf.

## Policy Sections

- **1. Policy Statement**
- **2. General**
- **3. Responsibilities**
- **4. Sanctions**

### 1. Policy Statement
Seneca upholds a learning community that values **academic integrity**, honesty, fairness, trust, respect, responsibility and courage.

### 2. General
Students should inform faculty at the earliest opportunity of any circumstance that may affect their academic performance. Should there be a suspected violation of this policy, the **academic integrity sanctions** will be applied according to the severity of the offence committed.

### 3. Responsibilities
All members of the Seneca community are responsible for upholding **academic integrity**. Students, faculty, and staff must report suspected violations.

### 4. Sanctions
Violations may result in **sanctions**, including grade penalties, suspension, or expulsion. Refer to Appendix B for the academic integrity sanctions.

## Appendix A: Academic Integrity Procedure

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Report the witnessing or suspicion of an offence | As soon as practically possible | Faculty, invigilator, student, staff |
| Inform the student of the suspected offence | As soon as practically possible | Faculty, invigilator, staff |
| Collect the assessment and related evidence | Within five days of such offence | Faculty, invigilator, staff |
| Commence the formal process if an offence has occurred | As soon as practically possible | Faculty |
| Deliver the Academic Integrity Report to the student | Within five days of such offence | Faculty |
| Submit student response to the report | Within five days from receipt | Student |
| Convene a meeting of the Academic Integrity Committee | Within 10 days from student's receipt | Academic Integrity Committee chair |
